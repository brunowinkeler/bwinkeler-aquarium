# Aquarium user guide

Children draw sea creatures on paper, a phone photographs them and they swim on the TV. The app
opens in Portuguese; the button at the top right switches to English (labels below are the English
ones).

## What you need

- A screen for the aquarium: open `https://aquarium.bwinkeler.com/tv/` in the TV or computer
  browser. The LG TV app comes later.
- One or more phones with a camera.
- White paper or a printed template, and crayons, markers or pencils.

## Start an aquarium on the TV

1. Open `/tv/` and choose **New aquarium**.
2. A QR code and a six-character code appear. The code lasts 10 minutes and the TV replaces it
   before it expires; several phones can use the same code.
3. Back (or Esc) opens the menu: show the code again, disconnect the phones, or end the aquarium.

## Join from a phone

1. Scan the QR code with the phone camera, or open `https://aquarium.bwinkeler.com` and type the
   code.
2. To keep the app on the home screen: on Android, accept the install prompt (or browser menu →
   Install app); on iPhone, Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

## Send a drawing

1. Draw a sea creature. Closed outlines are filled automatically, so an outline-only drawing keeps
   its white inside.
2. Choose **Take a photo** (or **Choose from the gallery**). Keep the sheet flat, in good light,
   without shadows over the drawing, and let it fill most of the photo.
3. On a plain sheet, drag the four dots to the corners of the paper. A printed template is
   straightened on its own and already picks the creature kind.
4. In the preview, pick the kind (fish, jellyfish, crab or turtle), flip or rotate it, and use
   **Adjustments** if parts are missing (sensitivity, close outlines, include loose parts). Add a
   name if you like and choose **Send to the TV**.
5. The aquarium holds 20 creatures; the oldest swims away when a new one arrives. **Creatures in
   this aquarium** lists them, and any phone of the aquarium can remove one.

## Printable templates

**Printable templates** (on the home screen, and on the join screen before joining) offers a
generic template and one per creature kind, as PDF. Print on A4 in landscape; scaling does not
matter. Draw inside the frame and leave the black corner squares uncolored: the app uses them to
find the sheet and the kind.

## When it ends

**End aquarium** on the TV ends it for every phone and deletes the drawings. If the TV only
disconnects, the aquarium waits 4 hours for it to come back.
